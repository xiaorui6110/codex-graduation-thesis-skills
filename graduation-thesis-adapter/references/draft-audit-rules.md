# Draft Audit Rules

## Goal

Audit an existing thesis draft before rewriting it.

## What To Detect

- template instructions still left in the manuscript
- placeholder headings such as `...`
- placeholder text such as `[待补充]`, `TODO`, `TBD`
- unsupported claims
- inconsistent chapter numbering
- mixed terminology such as `系统`, `平台`, `项目`, `应用` used without control

## Recommended Classification

- keep
- keep with minor edits
- rewrite
- delete

## Audit Output

When reporting audit findings, prefer section-based results instead of vague global comments.
