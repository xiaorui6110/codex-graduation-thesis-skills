# Detection Report Rewrite Playbook

## Goal

Rewrite hotspot sections indicated by similarity or AIGC reports while preserving project truth.

## Workflow

1. map hotspot pages to thesis sections
2. assign rewrite intensity (`light`, `medium`, `heavy`)
3. rewrite section logic, not just synonym replacement
4. keep facts stable and verifiable
5. repeat by hotspot priority until user target is met

## Constraints

- do not fabricate facts
- do not drop essential technical meaning
- prioritize high-yield sections first
